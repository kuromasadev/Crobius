# Crobius, the Half-World 

The event known as the “Dimensional Rift”, was a cataclysm that caused seven parallel dimensions of earth to merge into one. Excessive spectral and dark energy evaporated the Earth’s core, leaving the southern half of the planet’s land mass a nuclear fallout zone and completely severed from its other half.

Given the disruption of mass and space, interplanetary contact from Earth was no longer possible. Any interplanetary visitor would notice that there are multiple fragmented rings of debris, and that a half of Earth’s hemisphere consists of a dense, gaseous core. With seven dimensions condensed, gravity seemed to vary depending on which side of the planet one is occupying.

From the water and soil side of the half-world’s surface, the sky during the day seems a lot more translucent - a very cloudy day with many variable levels of brightness. There was still climate and weather - just not a clear indication of the sun itself. During the night, one can rarely spot where the Earth’s moon should be.

Religious deities, in general, were destroyed - or left more or less powerless to influence the half-world. Astral connections were severed and passage between other planes of existence became extremely difficult for interplanar travelers due to the risk of entering Earth on the gaseous hemisphere by accident. Due to the debris orbiting the planet, it became cost-prohibitive for the interplanar navigators to chart and figure out. The resulting coexistence of a handful of sentient races in the livable half-world emerged from self-preservation, at least at first.

Magic, spells and general arcana were still viable as long as the source of mana still remained being the life capacity of the planet. Human modern services and it’s dimensional derivatives such as the internet’s web service was interrupted for a few decades, until there were enough gliding stratospheric balloon “satellites” anchored to various locations in order to provide coverage. Any orbital satellites either made atmospheric re-entry, or got pulled into the Earth’s new rings.

While the event destroyed half of livable surface area, it also created a breeding ground for a new beginning of sentient life. Of the seven parallel dimensions, each had been occupied by a different “sentient species” (elves, dwarves, orcs, humans, tieflings, aasimar and dragon-like Xeel-Xar). Intermingled in the same setting, the future of the Half-World hinged on how much these societies were willing to cooperate with each other and coexist in a sustainable manner.

Post rift, the beings of the new half world naturally attempt to find their similarities, making encampments of sorts. It takes about 100 years for the settlements in the frindgelands to be called a Commonwealth of sorts, with species intermixing to create a diverse portside society. However, the nuclear fallout zone slowly was found to be spreading out, with the rift charge losing its strength to hold the two half atmospheres from blending. For the last few decades before the spirits arrive, the Frindgelands was experiencing serious toxicity with the air, losing whole settlements to fallout effects of radiation and lack of breathable air. The Fringeland Commonwealth (FLC) has been working an emergency fleet of refugee ships to transport the youngest and most resilient to the lands beyond the Great Sea. The peoples of FLC never had contact with those beyond the ocean, for it was deemed unnecessary to leave the fringelands for refuge in safer waters.

Inland, in the remaining northern hemisphere of Earth, the settlements turned into citadel's and metropolises, with the most northern regions finding itself with more resources that they had pre-rift. For most, the idea that the remaining Earth could turn into a toxic wasteland was quite inconceivable, for there had not been a way to observe Earth from outer orbit. Religion in the north morphed its way as a position of being pro-interspiece mix or being same-species only. For most, sightings of the largest species of all were very rare - it is rumored that these species resemble leviathans and beasts from the legends and keep to themselves near large mountain ranges or forests.
